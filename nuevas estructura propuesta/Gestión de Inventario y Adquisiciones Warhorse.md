In the Warhorse application workflow, **Inventory & Purchasing** (*Inventario y Compras*) is managed exclusively by the **Compras** user profile 1, 2\. It acts as the operational bridge between workshop maintenance needs and financial resource allocation.  
The sources outline a tightly structured inventory and procurement model designed around the following core pillars:

### 1\. The Work Order Gatekeeper (Essential Gateway)

A **Work Order (*Orden de Trabajo*) is a strict prerequisite for any purchasing activity**; a purchase requisition cannot exist in the system without one 3\.

* **Preventing Bottlenecks:** To resolve a previous operational bottleneck where purchases could not be made without an immediate, active repair, the workflow now includes **preventive work orders** (*preventivas*) 4\.  
* **Strategizing Stock:** This allows the workshop to register a preventive maintenance need to buy stock items for the inventory to maneuver parts for future use, even if there is no immediate unit repair required 2, 4\.

### 2\. The Three-Tiered Inventory Structure

The system tracks and categorizes inventory into three distinct streams 5:

* **Stock Inventory:** Available parts physically on hand that the workshop (*Taller*) can immediately select for requisitions instead of waiting for external orders 5, 6\.  
* **"Yonke" (Salvaged) Inventory:** Demolished or decommissioned parts stripped from retired units. Critically, these salvaged parts are **registered with a specific tag linking them directly to the unique ID of the unit** they originated from 2, 5\.  
* **External Purchases (*Compras Externas*):** Standard parts that are not available in existing stock or salvage and must be ordered from outside vendors 5\.

### 3\. The Modernized Purchasing Experience

For parts that must be sourced externally, the application transforms traditional procurement into a user-friendly digital flow 5:

* **Virtual Shopping Cart:** The purchasing agent's workspace is designed as a **"shopping cart" (*carrito de compra*) experience** where they can organize items by categories 5\.  
* **Supplier Directory (*Padrón de Proveedores*):** This interface is supported by an easily accessible directory of registered suppliers 5\.  
* **On-the-Fly Creation:** To keep operations agile, the purchasing agent can select from pre-registered vendors or create new supplier profiles directly inside the interface to build a smarter component selection system 5\.  
* **Integration:** All external purchases are strictly linked back to official **Purchase Orders (*Órdenes de Compra*)** and their originating Work Orders 5\.

### 4\. Off-Workflow Spending: Petty Cash (*Caja Chica*)

Not all transactions pass through the workshop's direct maintenance loop. The system designates a specific category for **miscellaneous purchases** (*compras misceláneas*) 7\.

* These non-maintenance expenses **are not added to the workshop's repair totals** 7\.  
* Instead, they are managed and tracked separately through a **Petty Cash (*Caja Chica*) reporting workflow** 7\.

### 5\. Oversight & Reporting

To close the loop, the data generated by these transactions feeds directly into the administrative dashboard 1, 6\. The **Administration** profile relies on two specific reports to maintain full visibility and financial transparency over this ecosystem 1, 6:

* **Inventory Reports** (*Reporte de Inventario*) 6
