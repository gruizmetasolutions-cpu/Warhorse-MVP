Within the **Warhorse application workflow**, reports serve as the primary mechanism for strategic oversight, providing the administration with the analytics needed to audit operations and ensure logistical efficiency 1\.  
The system relies on **four fundamental reports** and **one auxiliary report** to monitor every stage of the vehicle and purchasing lifecycle 2, 3:

### 1\. The Four Fundamental System Reports

To grant the administration full operational visibility, the system tracks data across four primary channels 1, 2:

* **Inventory Report (*Reporte de Inventario*):** This report monitors the fleet's parts and is divided into three distinct segments 2, 4\. It tracks standard **Stock** (parts on hand ready for workshop use), **"Yonke" (Salvaged) Inventory** (reclaimed parts specifically tagged to the unique unit ID they came from), and **External Purchases** that have been ordered to replenish inventory 4\.  
* **Purchasing Report (*Reporte de Compras*):** This logs all procurement activities and is fed directly by the purchasing department's digital "shopping cart" experience 2, 4\. It tracks external orders that are strictly justified by and linked to active or preventive Work Orders 4-6.  
* **Unit Health Report (*Reporte de Salud de las Unidades*):** This report monitors fleet availability by tracking the exact distribution of vehicles across the five operational unit states: **100% Active, Active with Warning(s), Inactive in Repair, Inactive \- Yonke**, and **Inactive/Decommissioned** 2, 7\.  
* **Inspection Report (*Reporte de Inspecciones*):** This catalogs the digital inspection questionnaires completed by drivers via yard tablets 2, 8\. This data is crucial because failed inspections act as the primary trigger for corrective maintenance work 5, 6\.

### 2\. The Auxiliary Report: Petty Cash (*Caja Chica*)

Outside the primary workshop maintenance stream, the system utilizes a dedicated report for **Miscellaneous Purchases (*Compras Misceláneas*)** 3:

* These minor transactions are tracked via a **Petty Cash (*Caja Chica*) report** 3\.  
* Crucially, these expenses **are not added to the workshop's direct repair totals**, ensuring that the main maintenance metrics remain clean and accurate 3\.

### How Reports Close the Operational Loop

These reports represent the final phase of the application workflow. By aggregating driver logs 8, workshop status updates 7, 9, and purchasing cart requisitions 4, they compile all raw operational data into actionable dashboards 1\. The **Administration** profile then uses this consolidated reporting suite to supervise the entire logistics chain from a single, centralized view 1, 2\.
