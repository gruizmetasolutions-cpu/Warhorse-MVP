The reclassification of the Warhorse application workflow is designed to audit and restructure operational processes to make them significantly more agile 1, 2\. Within this system, the overall workflow progresses through a sequential set of dependencies divided into **four key stages** 3-6:

### Stage 1: Vehicle Inspection (*Inspección*)

* **The Workflow Trigger:** The entire operational cycle begins with a digital inspection, which is a brief document completed by drivers (operators) upon leaving or arriving at the yard 3\.  
* **The Logging Process:** To access the system, operators use a tablet in the yard and log in using a QR code or their employee ID number 3\. They then complete a questionnaire linked directly to the inspection document 3\.  
* **System Alert:** If the driver logs any issues, the system automatically triggers an alert notifying the Workshop (*Taller*) department of the specific mechanical concern with the unit 4\.

### Stage 2: Operational Evaluation & Work Orders (*Órdenes de Trabajo*)

* **Intake and Review:** Upon receiving an inspection alert, the workshop team registers the entry in the system using a comprehensive form to detail the mechanical issues and determines if the vehicle requires an official Work Order (*Orden de Trabajo*) 4\.  
* **Two Types of Work Orders:**  
* **Corrective (*Correctiva*):** Generated directly from failed driver inspections to address immediate repair needs 5\.  
* **Preventive (*Preventiva*):** Created to manage general vehicle maintenance and strategically maneuver inventory 5, 7\. This pathway was specifically introduced to resolve a previous bottleneck where purchases could not be made without an active repair, allowing the team to buy stock items for future use without needing an immediate vehicle fix 5, 7\.  
* **Work Order Progress States:** Work orders transition through specific operational statuses 8:  
* *Activa:* The workshop is made aware of the order 8\.  
* *En proceso:* The repair is currently underway 8\.  
* *Liberada:* The repair is complete, removed from workshop records, and the vehicle returns to active status 8\.  
* *Liberada parcial:* The vehicle is released with a "Warning" (pending work) 8\. When work is resumed, the system links directly back to this pending order instead of forcing the creation of a new one 8\.  
* **Unit Health Categorization:** Based on these evaluations, unit health is classified into five distinct states: 100% Active, Active with Warning(s), Inactive in Repair (active but unavailable), Inactive as *Yonke* (salvage unit), and Inactive (fully decommissioned) 7, 8\.

### Stage 3: Procurement & Inventory (*Compras*)

* **A Mandatory Prerequisite:** A Work Order (either corrective or preventive) is a strict prerequisite for any purchasing activity; a purchase requisition cannot exist in the system without an active Work Order 4\.  
* **Inventory Segmentation:** Managed by the Purchasing department, inventory is classified into three channels 6, 7, 9:  
* *Stock:* Items already available for immediate workshop selection 6\.  
* *Yonke:* Salvaged parts tagged with the specific vehicle ID from which they originated 9\.  
* *External Purchases:* Standard parts sourced externally 9\.  
* **The Sourcing Experience:** External purchases are linked to official Purchase Orders (*Órdenes de Compra*) utilizing a directory of registered suppliers 9\. This is designed as a modern, virtual "shopping cart" experience where the buyer can select pre-registered vendors or create new ones on the fly 9\.  
* **Petty Cash (*Caja Chica*):** Any miscellaneous expenses not related to workshop repairs are handled outside the standard maintenance workflow via separate petty cash reports 10\.

### Stage 4: Closed-Loop Administration & Reporting (*Administración*)

* **Strategic Oversight:** The Administration profile oversees and monitors the entire lifecycle of these stages 1\.  
* **Total Visibility:** To close the loop and evaluate operational efficiency, the administrator relies on a central dashboard and the generation of four essential system reports: **Inventory, Purchases, Unit Health, and Inspections** 1, 6\.
